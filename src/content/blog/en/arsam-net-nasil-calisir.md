arsam.net is a marketplace that brings together unzoned or partially zoned land —
plots, fields, vineyards and olive groves — under a single catalogue. Listings from
private owners and verified offices speak the same language: price per square metre,
zoning status, deed type, road frontage, utilities and a verification badge.

## Start your search with a region

Rather than picking a province from a long list, typing a region name like "Çeşme" or
"Datça" usually returns faster, more relevant results. Region pages summarise the
average TL/m², the number of new listings in the last 30 days and a price trend at a
glance. From the left panel you can combine m², price, zoning and deed filters to
narrow your shortlist.

## Office verification and KVKK

The "Verified office" badge in the top-right corner means the office's chamber
registration, contract and KVKK privacy notice have been reviewed. When you send a
message to an office through a listing, only the data you submit is forwarded;
arsam.net does not retain your phone number or email for marketing purposes. The
**Privacy** menu under your account lets you erase your data at any time.

## Compare and favourite

Tapping the bird icon on a listing card adds it to your favourites; the compare
checkbox lets you place up to four listings side by side. The compare bar stays
visible until you navigate away from search; sharing the URL re-opens the same table
on the other side — handy when deciding with a partner or advisor.

## Publishing a listing

Owners and offices use the five-step wizard at `/ilan-ver`: location, plot details,
photos, price and KVKK consent. After publishing, listings can be edited, paused or
removed from your **Panel**.

We follow a single principle: a verified, comparable and transparent land marketplace.
