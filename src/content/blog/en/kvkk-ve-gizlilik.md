arsam.net is a Turkey-based platform and therefore falls under Law No. 6698 on the
Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). This article summarises, in plain language, which
data is processed for which purpose and which rights you can exercise. The full
privacy notice lives at [/kvkk](/kvkk).

## What data is collected?

- **Signup:** name, email, password hash and account role (buyer / seller / office).
- **Listing:** plot data (size, zoning, deed, photos), location hint and KVKK consent
  record.
- **Messages:** the message text sent from buyer to office, contact consent and
  timestamp.
- **Technical:** browser type, device type, anonymised traffic statistics.

We do **not** share third-party advertising identifiers; our cookie policy states this
explicitly.

## Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK every user can:

- Learn whether their data is being processed.
- Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
- Request deletion or destruction (the "erasure" request).
- Restrict processing.

To exercise these rights, the **My Account → Privacy** menu's "Delete my data" button
is enough. The request is started automatically; active records are erased within 30
days. Backups are cleaned up within 90 days.

## How are your messages forwarded to an office?

A message sent through a listing is forwarded only to the office owning that listing,
and only after KVKK consent is given. arsam.net does not process message content for
marketing purposes and does not share it with third parties.

## Cookie preferences

On first visit you see "Accept all" / "Strictly necessary" choices. Strictly necessary
cookies keep the session; analytics cookies are **opt-in** and can be disabled at any
time from "Settings → Cookies".

For questions and complaints, `kvkk@arsam.net` replies within 5 business days. That
commitment is co-signed by our technical team and our legal advisor.
